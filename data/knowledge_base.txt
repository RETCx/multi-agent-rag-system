TechCorp Solutions — Employee Handbook

International Travel Policy
TechCorp Solutions supports business travel for approved company purposes. All international travel must be pre-approved by the employee's direct manager and the HR department at least 10 business days in advance. Employees traveling internationally are required to submit a detailed itinerary including destination, purpose of travel, hotel accommodation, and estimated expenses. The company will cover economy-class airfare for flights under 8 hours and business-class airfare for flights exceeding 8 hours. Hotel accommodation is reimbursed up to $250 USD per night in standard cities and up to $400 USD per night in high-cost cities such as New York, London, Tokyo, and Singapore. Meals are reimbursed at a flat daily rate of $80 USD. Employees must retain all receipts and submit expense reports within 10 business days of returning. Travel insurance is provided automatically by TechCorp for all approved international trips. Employees must carry their corporate travel card for all travel-related expenses.

Domestic Travel Policy
Domestic travel within the country requires manager approval at least 5 business days in advance. Economy-class airfare and standard hotel accommodations up to $150 USD per night are covered. The daily meal allowance for domestic travel is $50 USD. Mileage reimbursement for personal vehicle use is provided at the current IRS standard rate. All domestic travel bookings must be made through the company's approved travel portal. Employees are encouraged to choose ground transportation over air travel for distances under 500 km when feasible, in alignment with the company's sustainability commitments.

Remote Work Policy
TechCorp Solutions embraces a hybrid work model. Full-time employees are eligible to work remotely up to 3 days per week after successfully completing their 90-day onboarding period. Remote work arrangements must be agreed upon with the employee's manager and documented in the HR system. Employees working remotely are expected to be available during core business hours from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone and must maintain a stable internet connection. The company provides a one-time home office stipend of $500 USD for eligible remote workers to cover equipment such as monitors, keyboards, and ergonomic accessories. Employees are responsible for maintaining a professional and distraction-free work environment during remote work hours. Full-remote arrangements of 5 days per week may be approved on a case-by-case basis for senior employees with demonstrated performance.

Paid Time Off Policy
All full-time employees receive 15 days of paid vacation per year, accrued at 1.25 days per month. After 3 years of service, PTO increases to 20 days per year. After 5 years, it increases to 25 days per year. Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days to the next calendar year. In addition, TechCorp observes 11 public holidays per year. Sick leave is provided separately at 10 days per year and does not roll over. Parental leave for primary caregivers is 16 weeks fully paid, and for secondary caregivers is 6 weeks fully paid. Bereavement leave of up to 5 days is available for immediate family members.

IT Equipment Policy
New employees receive a standard IT equipment package upon joining, which includes a company-issued laptop (MacBook Pro 14-inch or Dell XPS 15 based on role), a wired headset, and access to all company software licenses. Employees may request additional peripherals through the IT helpdesk. Equipment is the property of TechCorp Solutions and must be returned upon termination of employment. Lost or damaged equipment due to negligence may be subject to partial cost recovery. Software installation on company devices must be approved by the IT Security team. Employees must not use company laptops for personal activities that violate the Acceptable Use Policy.

Code of Conduct
TechCorp Solutions expects all employees to act with integrity, professionalism, and respect in all interactions. Discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Employees are expected to protect confidential company information and must sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement upon joining. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR immediately. Any suspected unethical behavior should be reported through the anonymous Ethics Hotline available on the company intranet. Retaliation against employees who report concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited.

Products and Services
TechCorp Solutions offers a suite of enterprise software products. TechCore ERP is a comprehensive enterprise resource planning platform designed for mid-to-large organizations covering financial management, supply chain, human resources, and procurement in a single integrated system with pricing starting at $5,000 per month for up to 100 users. DataSight Analytics is a business intelligence and analytics platform that enables real-time dashboards, predictive analytics, and AI-driven insights, integrating with over 200 data sources and available as cloud-hosted or on-premise with pricing starting at $2,000 per month. SecureID is an identity and access management solution providing single sign-on, multi-factor authentication, and role-based access control, certified SOC2 Type II and ISO 27001 with pricing starting at $8 per user per month with a minimum of 50 users. FlowDesk is a customer support and helpdesk platform with AI-powered ticket routing, SLA management, and a self-service knowledge base portal supporting integrations with Slack, Microsoft Teams, Salesforce, and Jira with pricing starting at $25 per agent per month.

Company Background
TechCorp Solutions was founded in 2012 and is headquartered in Austin, Texas, with regional offices in London, Singapore, and Sao Paulo. The company employs over 1,200 people worldwide across engineering, sales, customer success, and operations. TechCorp's mission is to empower organizations with intelligent and scalable technology solutions. The company is privately held and has raised $180 million in venture capital funding to date. TechCorp Solutions has been recognized as one of Deloitte's Technology Fast 500 companies for three consecutive years and was named a Great Place to Work in 2024.

Environmental and Sustainability Policy
TechCorp Solutions is committed to reducing its environmental footprint. The company has pledged to achieve carbon neutrality by 2030. All offices are powered by 100 percent renewable energy. Employees are encouraged to choose ground transportation over air travel for distances under 500 km when feasible. The company participates in an annual tree-planting initiative and matches employee donations to environmental nonprofits up to $500 per year. TechCorp has eliminated single-use plastics from all office facilities and provides recycling stations on every floor.

Data Security and Privacy Policy
All employees must complete mandatory data security training within 30 days of joining and annually thereafter. Customer data must be classified according to the company's data classification framework: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted. Access to Confidential and Restricted data requires manager approval and is logged by the security operations team. Employees must use company-approved tools for storing and transmitting sensitive data. Personal devices may not be used to access Restricted data without prior authorization from IT Security. All security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Center within 1 hour of discovery.
